HOW TO PLAY

Use the board as your spellbook.

Enemies advance in three lanes. Your guardians attack automatically. Match stars in the threatened lane to fire immediate magic: orange Flare stars deal damage, blue Tide stars freeze enemies, and green Bloom stars heal the citadel and rally its guardian.

Four or five matched stars create powerful novas and starfalls. Spend stardust between waves to permanently improve a lane's guardian.
