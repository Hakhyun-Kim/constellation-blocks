## Development

Constellation Defense was designed and developed by Hakhyun Kim with Codex as an AI development collaborator.

The current release still renders its visual game elements procedurally and synthesizes audio in the browser. Future image, audio, model, and animation assets are allowed when their source and commercial license are recorded here and in `assets/manifest.json`, and their download/rendering cost passes `npm run asset:check` plus the browser performance gate.
